PRE-OWNED CARS - PAARL | FREE AUTOMATED BUYER DISCOVERY

WHAT THIS DOES
- index.html: Public buyer form. A buyer sends their details to your WhatsApp after tapping Send in WhatsApp.
- dashboard.html: Public search shortcuts, automated public-search feed, local prospect tracker and CSV export.
- collector.py: Runs Google News RSS search queries for public buyer-request phrases. Results are unverified and often irrelevant; RSS indexes news, not every Facebook/classified listing.
- GitHub Actions: Runs the collector daily (subject to GitHub limits, outages and schedule delays).

HOW TO PUBLISH FOR FREE
1. Create a free GitHub account at https://github.com and a new PUBLIC repository called pre-owned-cars-paarl.
2. Upload ALL contents of this ZIP, preserving the .github/workflows/discover.yml folder. Do NOT upload actual customer information or your prospect CSV.
3. Under repository Settings > Pages, choose Deploy from a branch > main > /(root), Save.
4. Your public buyer URL will be https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/pre-owned-cars-paarl/
5. In repository Actions, open Public buyer discovery and select Run workflow once to populate discoveries.json. The scheduled job is configured for 06:15 UTC daily, and may run late or be skipped on inactive repositories.
6. Open dashboard.html locally for personal prospect notes. Its automated feed can load from the live site if you access the hosted dashboard, BUT do not enter customer personal data on a shared/public computer. Prefer local use and export CSV backups.
7. To load the automatic feed in a LOCAL dashboard, use the hosted dashboard for browsing results only, then keep a separate local copy for private prospect tracking; browser storage does not sync between these origins.

IMPORTANT
- GitHub Pages is public. NEVER upload your customer lead list or prospect CSV to GitHub.
- The buyer form does not automatically save leads to the dashboard. Buyers must click Send in WhatsApp; record follow-ups manually.
- Google News RSS does not reliably return personal buyer posts. This is automated discovery of public search results, NOT a guaranteed stream of qualified buyers.
- Only contact people through legitimate public contact channels, follow site rules, and comply with South Africa's POPIA/direct-marketing requirements. Obtain consent for marketing follow-ups.
- No paid hosting or API key required for this version. Free services have quotas and may change.
